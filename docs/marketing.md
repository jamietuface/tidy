### Short description (100 words)
Tidy makes two tedious tasks fast and satisfying: cleaning your camera roll and cutting wasteful app subscriptions. Swipe right to keep a photo, left to delete — just like Tinder. Tidy's AI groups blurry, duplicate, and old photos so you can make bulk decisions in seconds. The app tracker shows which of your installed apps you haven't opened in weeks and what they're costing you. Built for iPhone with Apple design throughout. Available on iOS with Android coming soon. Free to download with a Pro subscription for unlimited access.
