# Tidy — Business Plan

## The Problem
The average iPhone has 2,000+ photos. People hate deleting them. They also pay for apps they haven't opened in months. Nobody does anything about either — it's too tedious.

## The Solution
Tidy makes both effortless:
1. **Swipe to clean** — left to delete, right to keep. Like Tinder for your camera roll.
2. **AI grouping** — automatically surface blurry, duplicate, and old photos so you can kill batches in seconds.
3. **App tracker** — shows your subscriptions and last-used dates. One tap to cancel what you don't need.

## Target Market
- **Primary**: iPhone users aged 18–35 who are storage-stressed and subscription-fatigued
- **Secondary**: Android users (Phase 2)
- **Market size**: 1.5B+ smartphone users, ~60% have complained about storage at least once

## Business Model

### Revenue projections (Year 1)
- Target: 10,000 downloads in first 3 months
- Conversion rate: 5% free → paid

- **Year 1 ARR target: £9,000**

### Growth levers
- Viral loop: "Tidy freed 2.3GB — share your results"
- Referral: 1 month free for each friend who converts
- App Store featuring: photo utility category is underserved

## Go-to-Market

### Phase 1 — Launch (Month 1–3)
- App Store launch (iOS first)
- TikTok / Instagram Reels: screen recordings of satisfying swipe sessions
- Reddit: r/apple, r/productivity, r/personalfinance (app tracker angle)
- ProductHunt launch

### Phase 2 — Growth (Month 4–12)
- Android launch
- Referral programme
- Partnership with storage-related YouTube channels
- Apple Search Ads

### Phase 3 — Scale (Year 2)
- B2B: white-label for mobile carriers
- Tidy for Teams: shared photo cleanup for families/businesses
- Tidy AI: on-device model for offline grouping
